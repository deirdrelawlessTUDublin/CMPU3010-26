Week 1

What are we doing this week?

This week introduces the module and reviews the database and SQL knowledge that we will build on during the semester.

We will also set up PostgreSQL and begin working with the CommunityCare database, which will be used as a common case study throughout the module.

Lecture Notes

Week 1: From Databases 1 to Databases 2
In the Week 1 lab, you will review the relational database and SQL skills developed in Databases 1 using the CommunityCare database.

During the lecture, we will use the CommunityCare database and your experience of the lab to introduce the main themes of Databases 2 and consider how this module builds on your existing database knowledge.

We will also discuss the module learning outcomes, indicative syllabus and assessment strategy.
